## 2.1.0 · 2026-11-21 · The first changelog
The first entry. It sums up the latest work; earlier changes are in `git log`.

### New
- **Blocks break into pieces** you can carry and build with: slabs, planks and cubes, placed on their own or in buildings.
- **Buildings redrawn,** with plank and stone-slab walls, a plank fence for the farm plot, and a new Blacksmith. Settlers pay for a building piece by piece as they build it.
- **A hotbar of 4 slots** you fill from the Bag by dragging.
- **Settlers, monsters and animals find their way** around obstacles.
- **Settlers fell trees and forage** near camp, and farms plant with seeds.
- **Gear shows on your character,** and sealing your first rift earns a cape.
- **A new look** for the HUD, menus and icons, carved from stone and wood.
- **Send Feedback** from the pause menu or the start screen, with an optional screenshot.
- **Phones held sideways** get a compact HUD.
- **Rifts are bigger and rarer,** and send reinforcements while you close them.

### Fixed
- Settlers no longer lose the load they were hauling.
- Esc closes Help, and aiming and reach feel right again.
