## Decided

All earlier questions are resolved. The decisions live where the work does:

- **Phase 3:** [`PHASE_3_DETAILED_PLAN.md` → Decisions](phases/PHASE_3_DETAILED_PLAN.md#decisions), 1–38. Decisions 13–38 (November 21) add the world and endgame: a finite island of 4–5 territories, three rift tiers around a heart rift, resurgence that cascades from small to large rifts, outposts and patrols, peaceful builder mode in Phase 3, slow guard weapon wear with Blacksmith repair, the freed island or New Game+ after the heart is sealed, a single-landmass island, what New Game+ keeps, where the companion is found, outposts that grow on their own, free travel by rift waystones (roads in Phase 5), separate stockpiles per settlement, and New Game+ on the same island: every rift reopens, blocks under the reopened rifts break, and each cycle adds one more difficulty mechanic in a set order. Reopened rifts move to a new spot in their tier's zone, possibly inside a settlement, and nothing is protected from them. Corruption wears down player-built blocks by material, and settlers whose campfire is destroyed move to the nearest outpost or wait for a new one.
- **Roadmap:** [`3D_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` → Roadmap Decisions](3D_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md#roadmap-decisions-november-21-2026): Phase 3 before the companion, an ongoing basics track (saves, settings, audio, accessibility), mobile first-class, multiplayer kept lightly with the unused action middleware removed.
