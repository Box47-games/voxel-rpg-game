## Decided

All earlier questions are resolved. The decisions live where the work does:

- **Phase 3:** [`PHASE_3_DETAILED_PLAN.md` → Decisions](phases/PHASE_3_DETAILED_PLAN.md#decisions), 1–26. Decisions 13–26 (November 21) add the world and endgame: a finite island of 4–5 territories, three rift tiers around a heart rift, resurgence that cascades from small to large rifts, outposts and patrols, peaceful builder mode in Phase 3, slow guard weapon wear with Blacksmith repair, the freed island or New Game+ after the heart is sealed, a single-landmass island, what New Game+ keeps, where the companion is found, outposts that grow on their own, and travel by rift waystones (roads in Phase 5).
- **Roadmap:** [`3D_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` → Roadmap Decisions](3D_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md#roadmap-decisions-november-21-2026): Phase 3 before the companion, an ongoing basics track (saves, settings, audio, accessibility), mobile first-class, multiplayer kept lightly with the unused action middleware removed.
